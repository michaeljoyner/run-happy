My first Jeffing race - a 10km in Durban - and I took 9 minutes off my previous 10km race. It takes a bit of discipline to start off with the required Run-Walk-Run intervals, walking while the pack is all running and jostling for a bit of open road but you are certainly rewarded in the second half with legs that are still fresh.

<img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dy6grlu8z/image/upload/v1788513308/ult4vhhy2myzn6klyue8.jpg" alt="">

I did learn something though - never forget your hat - it hides hair that has definitely seen better days! It also prevents squinting and looking down which appears to cause a display of neck wrinkles. But hey we’re there for the running and in my mind I am still golden and smooth, muscles rippling, blonde ponytail flowing out the back.

So now for the next project or two. I am eyeing a 21,1km in Umhlanga Rocks on the 15th November so that I can see what a disciplined Run-Walk-Run approach can do over that distance. I’m hoping it’s not too hilly - perhaps I should take a peek at the route profile.

I am also working my way through Allie Bailey’s “31 Days”. If you don’t know of Allie you absolutely must read “There Is No Wall”. Check her out [here](https://www.alliebailey.co.uk/about)

<img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dy6grlu8z/image/upload/v1788513308/sasdh3dblic8rfuncxti.jpg" alt="">

Back to “31 Days”. Each chapter should be read in a day and then there is homework in a workbook. This ensures that I don’t skim through the book without thinking deeply.

We self-sabotage all the time when we set out to achieve something as our brains seek to conserve energy, avoid pain and pursue pleasure. My most common self-sabotaging thought is “you can’t run/walk that far - why don’t I change the plan and split the distance into two workouts?” Or “I can’t have just one small glass of wine before a race or long run - I may as well have two or three”. Both thoughts are utter bullshit - that damn brain is trying to avoid pain and pursue pleasure. And I do know that running a good race or completing a long run will give me way more pleasure.

So I’m hoping that by the end of 31 days I will be a mental strength ninja. I’d like to say watch this space but I’m really glad that no one can see into my brain - I may end up in an institution for mad old goats.
